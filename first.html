Fitness and Healthy Living: A Complete Guide

In today’s fast-paced world, fitness is no longer just a trend—it has become a necessity. Sedentary lifestyles, long working hours, and processed foods are taking a heavy toll on overall health.

What True Fitness Means

Fitness is not limited to building six-pack abs or lifting heavy weights. True fitness means having the endurance, strength, and flexibility to perform daily activities efficiently.

The Four Pillars of Fitness

Physical fitness relies on cardiovascular endurance, muscular strength, and joint flexibility achieved through regular movement.
