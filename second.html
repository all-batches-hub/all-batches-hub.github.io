Varanasi: The Spiritual and Cultural Capital of India

Situated on the sacred banks of the Ganges River in Uttar Pradesh, Varanasi—also known as Kashi or Benares—is recognized as one of the oldest continually inhabited cities in the world.

1. Historical and Religious Importance

Varanasi holds immense significance in Hindu mythology and philosophy. Believed to have been founded by Lord Shiva thousands of years ago.

Jaipur: The Historic Pink City of Rajasthan

Located in the heart of Rajasthan, Jaipur—popularly known as the 'Pink City'—serves as the vibrant capital of India's most regal state.

Goa: The Coastal Paradise of Sun, Sand, and Heritage

Located along India’s southwestern coast on the Arabian Sea, Goa is the country's smallest state by area but one of its most popular travel destinations.
